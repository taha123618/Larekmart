# Synthesized Project Context

## Overview

**Larekmart** is a production-grade microservices e-commerce platform for market place like: amazon,daraz,ALiexpress,ALibaba and other , built with a pnpm + Turborepo monorepo. It features two Next.js 15 frontends (`client` storefront and `admin` control portal), 15 polyglot backend microservices (Node.js, Go, Python), and 11 shared packages.

## Target Audience & Personas

- **Customers**: Retail shoppers browsing marketplace products, selecting variants, adding to cart, and checking out securely via Stripe.
- **Administrators**: E-commerce operations staff managing product catalog, tracking orders, reviewing revenue analytics, and managing user roles.
